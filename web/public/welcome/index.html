Real game, 30 second loop

How it plays

Connect a GitHub repo and it becomes a floating island. Every district is a folder. Everything you see is real work happening in your repo.

1 · Ore

Feedback is ore

Issues, Sentry errors and PostHog drops get mined, sorted and planned. Your Claude and ChatGPT sign-ins power the machines that build the fix.

2 · Gauntlet

Every PR runs the gauntlet

CI, a verify bay, AI review, your merge gate, then orbit watch after it ships. Anything that fails rides the rework belt back with a scar tag.

3 · Launch

A rocket is a merged PR

Launch merges the pull request for real. The bottleneck moves, and there is always a next move waiting for you.

Why it exists

You're the bottleneck. That's the game.

AI writes the code now. What slows shipping is a person deciding what to build, what to check, and what it has to prove. Shiptorio makes those calls visible and fast, then pushes you further: add more checks, verify everything, and let each clean ship earn trust. Verify, then automate. Over time the factory runs more and more of itself.
